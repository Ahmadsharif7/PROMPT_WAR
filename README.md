# ProjectMatch // Synthesis

> **Next-generation compatibility intelligence and team synthesis platform for hackathons, startups, research initiatives, and open-source ventures.**

ProjectMatch doesn't just match keywords — it calculates multi-dimensional compatibility and synthesizes high-performance, complementary squads with full domain coverage.

---

## 1. Problem

Finding the right teammates for high-stakes projects (hackathons, 0-to-1 startups, research labs) is broken:
- **Keyword searching is shallow**: Two people who both know "Python" might have completely incompatible weekly schedules or conflicting domain interests.
- **Similarity vs. Complementarity**: Great teams require diverse roles (Frontend Architecture, Machine Learning, UI/UX Ergonomics, Backend Reliability), not 4 developers with identical skills.
- **Black-box scores lack trust**: Seeing "85% match" with no explanation fails to help builders understand *why* they should work together.

---

## 2. Solution

**ProjectMatch // Synthesis** transforms team assembly into an intelligent command center:
1. **Multi-Vector Compatibility Signal**: Real-time weighted evaluation of skills, weekly bandwidth, domain alignment, and experience.
2. **Explainability Engine**: Transparent reasons explaining why two builders or a project match (e.g. *"Strong overlap in React & UI/UX + 18h/wk sprint capacity + shared focus on Spatial Computing"*).
3. **Team Synthesis Matrix**: Visual role composition analyzer that detects squad gaps and recommends complementary builders to complete 100% squad readiness.
4. **Proprietary Ambient Glass Interface**: Precision engineering aesthetic built with dark-mode glass surfaces, ambient atmospheric illumination, and tactile micro-interactions.

---

## 3. Matching Algorithm & Explainability

The **ProjectMatch Compatibility Engine** uses a deterministic, multi-vector weighted model:

$$\text{Final Score} = (\text{Skills} \times 0.45) + (\text{Interests} \times 0.20) + (\text{Availability} \times 0.20) + (\text{Experience} \times 0.15)$$

### Vector Breakdown:
- **Skills Coverage & Quality (45%)**: Computes set overlap between candidate skill inventory and project required stack, blended with skill proficiency (1–100) and categorized complementary skills.
- **Domain Interests (20%)**: Semantic & tag overlap across project mission domains (e.g. AI Interfaces, Clean Energy, Spatial Computing).
- **Weekly Availability Bandwidth (20%)**: Compares the builder's actual weekly sprint capacity against the project's target weekly hours needed.
- **Experience Seniority Closeness (15%)**: Measures level alignment across *Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Lead* tiers.

### Configurable Engine Presets:
- **Standard Synthesis** (45 / 20 / 20 / 15)
- **48h Hackathon Sprint** (35 / 15 / 40 / 10) — Prioritizes sprint availability
- **Deep Tech & Research** (50 / 20 / 10 / 20) — Prioritizes technical mastery
- **Early-Stage Startup** (40 / 30 / 15 / 15) — Prioritizes domain vision & product execution

## 4. Key Features

- **Live Persona Switcher**: Instantly switch between 10 realistic builders (e.g. *Maya Patel*, *Aarav Sharma*, *Arjun Mehta*, *Nisha Gupta*) to watch compatibility scores and explanations recalculate in real-time.
- **Circular Animated Compatibility Ring**: Smooth count-up progress SVG with tier-colored glows and completion micro-pulses.
- **5-Step Project Setup Wizard**: Step-by-step guidance calculating a live **Project Readiness Index** before publishing.
- **Dual Queue Requests**: Timeline-based *Sent* and *Received* join requests with real-time Accept/Decline state persistence.
- **Capability Telemetry Profile**: Interactive skill proficiency adjustments, domain tag clouds, and custom weekly availability slider.
- **Team Synthesis Matrix**: Role coverage tree showing assigned leads and top candidate recommendations for open positions.

## 8. Assumptions & Design Philosophy

1. Deterministic weighted scoring is used to provide transparent, explainable compatibility without black-box hallucination.
2. Realistic seed data is provided across 10 diverse builder personas and 5 multi-disciplinary projects.
3. The design adheres strictly to the **ProjectMatch // Synthesis** anti-slop aesthetic: no generic purple gradients or AI chat gimmicks, but rather a focused, high-density aerospace/systems engineering command workspace.
